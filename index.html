Enter the world of Shadow Yoddha

Shadow Yoddha: The Eternal Run is a free browser action runner. Guide a warrior through a dangerous ancient world, leap over obstacles, collect coins and face elite enemies. Build your score, master your timing and try to beat your saved best run.

How to play

On desktop, use Space, Up Arrow or W to jump; press X or J to attack, Q for Shadow Power, E for Shadow Phase, and P or Escape to pause. On supported touch devices, use the on-screen Jump, Attack, Power and Phase controls. A second jump is available while airborne.

What makes each run different

Watch for hazards, collect glowing coins and manage your abilities. Elite enemy encounters interrupt the run and require careful timing. Your best score and selected sound and effects preferences are saved locally in your browser on this device.
