The Eight Realms of Shadow Yoddha

Campaign map · 8,000 levels · Three enemy victories per level

The campaign progression system is organised as eight consecutive realms. Each realm spans 1,000 levels, and each level is cleared by defeating three encounter enemies. The campaign panel displays the current level, the realm name, the number of enemies defeated in the current level and a difficulty indicator.

The realm names draw on a fantasy interpretation of lokas and elemental environments. They are fictional settings within Shadow Yoddha's game world, not a guide to religious belief or a representation of any living tradition.

1. Agni Lok — Levels 1–1,000

Theme: Fire Kingdom. Realm guardian: Agni Asura. This is the opening realm in the campaign sequence. It introduces the player to the level-and-encounter rhythm before the later realms.

2. Vana Lok — Levels 1,001–2,000

Theme: Living Forest. Realm guardian: Vana Rakshak. The forest realm shifts the campaign's atmosphere from the opening fire kingdom to a setting imagined around dense, living wilderness.

3. Nag Lok — Levels 2,001–3,000

Theme: Serpent Kingdom. Realm guardian: Nagraj. This chapter uses serpent imagery as its fantasy identity and marks the third thousand-level section of the progression map.

4. Him Lok — Levels 3,001–4,000

Theme: Frozen Kingdom. Realm guardian: Him Asura. The frozen realm is the campaign's cold-environment chapter.

5. Vajra Lok — Levels 4,001–5,000

Theme: Thunder Kingdom. Realm guardian: Vajra Dev. Its theme is built around storms, thunder and force.

6. Patal Lok — Levels 5,001–6,000

Theme: Demon Kingdom. Realm guardian: Patal Rakshas. This section takes the campaign into its darker underworld-inspired fantasy setting.

7. Maya Lok — Levels 6,001–7,000

Theme: Illusion Kingdom. Realm guardian: Maya Samrat. The seventh realm is themed around illusion and uncertainty.

8. Shadow Lok — Levels 7,001–8,000

Theme: Shadow Dimension. Realm guardian: Shadow Emperor. The final thousand-level realm is the destination of the campaign progression system.

How level progression works

Defeating an encounter enemy increments the defeated-enemy count. After the third victory, the game displays a level-completion screen and begins a three-second transition. The next level then loads and the defeated-enemy count returns to zero. The campaign state is stored locally in the browser under its own progression key, separately from the best-run score.

The progression code defines the realm sequence, level ranges, guardian names and a difficulty indicator that rises with level and is capped at 100. These entries describe the campaign data currently present in the project; they should not be read as a claim that every realm has a distinct playable environment or unique boss animation in the current browser build.
