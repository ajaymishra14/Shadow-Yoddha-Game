How to Play

Learn the controls before beginning your journey.

Desktop controls

  • Jump: Space, ↑ or W. A second jump is available while airborne.
  • Sword attack: X or J.
  • Shadow Power: Q.
  • Shadow Phase: E.
  • Pause: P or Escape.

Phone and tablet controls

  • Tap JUMP to jump. Tap again in the air for a double jump.
  • Tap ATTACK to swing your sword during combat.
  • Tap POWER to activate Shadow Power.
  • Tap PHASE to activate Shadow Phase.

Tips

  • Watch the ground ahead and time your jumps to clear obstacles.
  • Collect glowing coins to increase your run score and coin total.
  • Save your abilities for difficult hazards and enemy encounters.
  • During an enemy encounter, watch the enemy's warning animation and avoid incoming attacks.
  • Your best score is saved locally in your browser on that device. Clearing browser storage may erase it.
