About Shadow Yoddha

The Eternal Run

Shadow Yoddha is an original browser-based action runner built around a lone warrior travelling through a dark, ancient world. The game combines fast running, timed jumps, collectible coins, sword attacks and supernatural shadow abilities.

What you can play

  • Endless-run gameplay with a score and a saved best score on your device.
  • Coins to collect while avoiding hazards.
  • Combat encounters against elite enemies.
  • Shadow Power and Shadow Phase abilities.
  • Keyboard controls on desktop and touch controls on supported phones and tablets.

Designed for quick sessions

Start a run, learn the timing of each obstacle, use your abilities carefully and try to beat your personal best. The browser game runs directly on this website; no account is required to play.
