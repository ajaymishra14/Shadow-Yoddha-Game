Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Shadow Yoddha website handles information when you visit and play the browser game. It should be reviewed and updated whenever the website adds analytics, advertising partners, contact forms or other services.

Information stored by the game

Shadow Yoddha currently uses browser local storage to remember gameplay preferences and progress on the device you use. This may include your best score, campaign level and progress, sound preference and visual-effects preference. This information is stored in your browser and is not, through the game code alone, sent to a Shadow Yoddha game account or game server.

Cookies and advertising

The website currently includes the Google AdSense JavaScript library with a configured publisher identifier. Whether advertisements are displayed depends on the publisher account, Google's site review and the account's ad settings; the presence of code on the page does not mean the site has been approved or that ads are currently being served. When Google advertising services are used, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure and personalize advertisements, subject to the provider's settings and applicable law. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons to collect information as a result of ad serving on this website.

For information about Google's advertising data practices, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services

This website may load third-party services such as Google advertising scripts and may link to third-party websites, such as GitHub. Those providers and websites operate under their own privacy policies. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review the relevant provider policies for details about their data handling.

Children's privacy

The website is intended for a general audience and does not intentionally ask players to submit personal information to play. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through a service linked from this website, contact the project maintainer through the issue tracker so the matter can be reviewed.

Your choices

You can clear this game's locally stored preferences and scores by clearing the website's site data in your browser; doing so may erase your saved game progress on that device. You can manage advertising personalization through the controls provided by the relevant advertising provider. Where required by law or applicable platform rules, a consent mechanism will be provided before relevant advertising technologies are used.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or service providers change. The latest version will be published on this page with its revision date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the Shadow Yoddha issue tracker. Please do not post sensitive personal information publicly.

Before enabling or expanding advertising, confirm the actual advertising and consent providers in use and configure any legally required consent mechanism for your audience.
